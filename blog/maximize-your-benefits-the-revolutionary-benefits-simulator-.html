What is a Benefits Simulator?

A Benefits Simulator is an online tool that helps you estimate your eligibility for government benefits, such as Universal Credit, Tax Credits, or Council Tax Support. By answering a series of questions, you can get an idea of which benefits you might be entitled to and how much you could receive.

Potential Features of a Benefits Simulator

A Benefits Simulator typically includes the following features:

* Benefits calculator: Estimates your eligibility for various government benefits based on your income, circumstances, and other factors. * Benefits eligibility check: Provides a comprehensive assessment of your eligibility for different benefits, including any conditions or restrictions that may apply. * Benefit amounts calculator: Helps you estimate the amount of benefits you might receive, taking into account factors like your income, expenses, and family circumstances. * Application guidance: Offers step-by-step guidance on how to apply for benefits, including tips on what documents you'll need and how to submit your application. * Claim tracking: Keeps track of your benefit claims, including the status of your application and any follow-up actions you need to take.
